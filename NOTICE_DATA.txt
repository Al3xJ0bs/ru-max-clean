RU Max Clean 4.9 — notice for source data
==========================================

The builder code and the linguistic/encyclopedic data are separate works.

Main online sources used by MAX mode:

1. Russian Wiktionary / Kaikki machine-readable extraction
   https://kaikki.org/ruwiktionary/
   Content originates from Wikimedia Wiktionary and is subject to the applicable
   Wikimedia/Wiktionary content licenses (including Creative Commons terms).

2. Wikidata Lexeme entity dump
   https://dumps.wikimedia.org/wikidatawiki/entities/latest-lexemes.json.bz2
   Wikidata structured data is released under CC0.

3. Russian Wikipedia pages-articles dump
   https://dumps.wikimedia.org/ruwiki/latest/ruwiki-latest-pages-articles.xml.bz2
   Wikipedia text is used only as a filtered fallback definition layer and is
   subject to CC BY-SA and applicable Wikimedia terms. Redistribution of a
   generated dictionary containing this text must preserve required attribution
   and share-alike conditions.

4. OpenCorpora morphology
   https://opencorpora.org/
   Used for lookup-form links only, never as displayed definition prose.

5. V. I. Dal StarDict archive
   Download mirrors are listed in build_ru_max_clean.py. It is used as a
   historical fallback layer.

6. Optional reader layers
   latin_wiktionary.tsv and literary_wiktionary.tsv are conservative extracts
   from the local Russian Wiktionary/Kaikki dump and retain Russian glosses
   only. The curated seed TSV files are maintained with this project. The
   applicable Wiktionary/Kaikki and project licensing/attribution terms must
   be reviewed before redistribution of the companion packs.

RU Max Clean never puts source labels/credits inside the small KOReader popup
article because the user-facing format is definition-only. Build/source metadata
is written separately to RU-Max-Clean/BUILD_INFO.json and SOURCES.txt.

Before redistributing a generated dictionary, review and comply with the current
licenses of every source enabled in that build.
